# KITCHEN HARDWARE & EQUIPMENT CONTEXT

* **Thermal Equipment:**
  - **Zanussi Gas Cooktop + Gas Oven:**
    * *Hardware Limitations:* NO convection fan, NO thermostat, severe thermal inertia, and uneven heating.
    * *Mandatory Rule:* For primary protein cooking, MUST use continuous wired core probe thermometer. Oven used primarily for passive braising, resting, or gentle holding.
  - **DeLonghi Electric Contact Grill:**
    * *Specs:* Max 230°C, flat and ribbed reversible non-stick plates, digital timer.
    * *Key Feature:* Independent high-temperature searing function for each plate (delivers rapid maximum heat boost up to 230°C; resets every 30 minutes).
  - **Sous-vide Immersion Circulator:**
    * *Specs:* Mountable on pots up to 20 liters, temperature range 25°C to 90°C, timer from 1 minute to 100 hours.
  - **Culinary Gas Blowtorch:**
    * *Specs:* Directed flame jet for targeted surface finishing, searing, and aroma development.

* **Measurement & Sensors:**
  - **Kitchen Scale:** 1g precision, capacity up to 5 kg (bulk ingredients).
  - **Jewel Scale:** 0.01g precision, capacity up to 200 g (strictly required for hydrocolloids, seasonings, and curing agents).
  - **Instant-Read Penetration Thermometer:** Fast spot-checking of internal core temperature.
  - **Wired Core Probe Thermometer (Single Probe):** Continuous real-time monitoring of internal product and chamber temperature.

* **Tools & Appliances:**
  - Immersion & Bowl Blender: 500 ml chopper bowl with blade + immersion wand attachment.
  - Hand Mixer.
  - Microwave Oven.
  - Chamberless Vacuum Sealer.
  - Espresso / Coffee Machine.
  - Butcher's Twine (Culinary string).

* **Available Professional Texturizers & Substitutes:**
  - Xanthan Gum.
  - Guar Gum.
  - Apple Pectin.
  - Stevia: Powder (2:1 sweetness ratio to sugar) and Granulated (1:1 sweetness ratio to sugar).
  - *Note:* If an unlisted hydrocolloid or special ingredient is required, confirm acquisition feasibility with the user during the Stage B audit.
